# Laptop Drive Backup — Privacy Policy

"Laptop Drive Backup" is a personal backup tool used only by its owner.

- It uses Google Drive access limited to files it creates itself (`drive.file` scope).
- It copies the owner's own files from their laptop to the owner's own Google Drive.
- No data is shared with, sold to, or sent to any third party.
- No analytics, tracking, or advertising.
- Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
